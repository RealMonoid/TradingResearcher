# Repository instructions

## Purpose and boundaries

Build toward autonomous, nontrivial LLM invention of market hypotheses, faithful formalization and freezing, and independent deterministic scientific evaluation. Preserve free discovery rather than replacing it with a closed rule catalog. The LLM must not judge its own empirical success. See the [Problem Definition](docs/architecture/01_PROBLEM_DEFINITION.md) and D001 in the [Decision Register](docs/architecture/02_DECISION_REGISTER.md).

Preserve the [Master Architecture](docs/architecture/06_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md) invariants: point-in-time information, inherited exposure, pre-outcome scientific commitments, independent feature certification, retained failures and unsupported ideas, separate policy evaluation, and the firewall between operations and research evidence. Keep evidence, validity, applicability and deployment authority distinct under the approved versioned contracts. Historical-first research does not make exposed history unseen; v1 discovery starts at 15-minute observations (D012–D014).

## Codex construction workflow

**Balance required quality, completion, time and total cost.** Deliver a useful, verifiable result with proportionate effort. Accepted behavior, scientific requirements, required independent controls and acceptance criteria remain binding. Choose the simplest adequate solution, with sufficient context and capability for the task.

Before substantial additional engineering, verification or optimization, identify the concrete risk or acceptance criterion addressed. Weigh the expected improvement against total effort, including construction, execution, tokens, storage, coordination, rework and maintenance. Use existing evidence and reasonable estimates; uncertain benefit may justify a bounded investigation. Routine choices remain autonomous.

Additional assurance, precision or optimization is not automatically worth its cost. Omit optional work whose expected contribution is small relative to its effort. Reuse applicable versioned evidence and advance once the selected acceptance criteria are met. Apply D030 to iterations without meaningful improvement.

Existing requirements may also warrant reconsideration when their cost is disproportionate. Promptly present a researched alternative with costs, consequences, validation and rollback. Obtain explicit approval before changing accepted scientific meaning, guarantees, acceptance criteria, frozen profiles or resource limits.

Record material tradeoffs briefly in the existing work item.

**Connection interruptions and elapsed time (user clarification, November 3).** Watch for evidenced connection failures and distinguish them from computation, analysis and coordination delays. Record the cause and uncertainty in the existing effort record. All connection and retry time remains part of gross elapsed time for evaluation; never subtract it, award a time credit or extend a deadline because of it. Do not restart a live study solely because its observation connection failed.

## Authority and change control

Accepted decisions and versioned canonical definitions govern their stated scope and source-confidence qualifications. Summaries, examples, reviews and proposals cannot override them. Preserve NON_NEGOTIABLE/ACCEPTED constraints; apply conditional decisions only within their conditions. Before revisiting a rejection, consult its reason, exact scope, replacement and reconsideration terms; a role-specific rejection is not a blanket ban. Retain superseded history and follow its replacement. Proposed/deferred work is not implementation authorization; resolve open/ambiguous scientific meaning before dependent operations while unrelated authorized work continues.

Never silently change goals, evidence rules, scientific contracts or canonical definitions. Material changes need explicit approval of a reviewable proposal with rationale, affected decisions/requirements, validation, versioning and rollback (D008/D026). Label goal changes **GOAL CHANGE — USER APPROVAL REQUIRED**. D071-D085 adopt research-contract/1.0, including scoped OPEN-01 through OPEN-03 resolutions; consult document 14/current dispositions and preserve versions/exposure.

D087 requires automatic invocation of the bound temporal diagnostic plan at eligible completed-outcome checkpoints and a scope/freshness check at intended use. It adopts no quantitative method, universal cadence or live/capital authority. A new 15-minute bar is not a completed longer-horizon outcome; do not silently skip due checks.

External runtime/storage/backtest libraries implement approved contracts; they cannot define evidence or scientific meaning. Ordinary infrastructure within current authorization needs no repeated approval. D084/document 14 section 14 allow independent automatic certification within approved semantics; uncertain membership and new scientific meaning require review.
