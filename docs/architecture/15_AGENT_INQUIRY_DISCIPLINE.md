## Working rule

### 1. Identify the investigation object

State what is being examined: a market assumption, its formalization, a measurement or data property, software behavior, or the framework specification. Keep the object's source/version and the scope of the conclusion explicit. Do not silently substitute a general essay, another model's summary or a related component for the actual object.

Treat externally supplied questions, shared premises and the agent's own framing as candidates for examination. Check interpretations against the relevant permitted source artifacts, including how those artifacts were produced and their known limitations. A canonical decision governs current behavior but remains open to explicitly labeled criticism; criticism alone does not change it.

### 2. Explain what the investigation could clarify

Before a targeted investigation, state the uncertainty and which possible findings could change the assessment or next action. Indirect clarification counts: checking a measurement, testing a prerequisite, identifying an applicability boundary or discovering that the original question is unsuitable.

Open exploration may begin with an honest search purpose rather than a finished hypothesis, competing explanations or a predicted useful result. Preserve changes in purpose in the search trajectory. Do not invent a hypothesis merely to justify exploration or require one decisive question for every discovery path. A stated purpose grants no additional data or tool access.

### 3. Challenge assumptions without requiring an objection

The challenger examines which consequential assumptions were inherited without examination, which concrete counterexamples or alternative relationships could weaken the claim, and whether the proposed check could reveal them. The task framing itself may be questioned. "No substantiated objection found" is an acceptable result and is not evidence that the candidate is true.

Challenges remain proposals grounded in permitted information. They cannot assign scientific status, silently replace H_idea, reveal another discovery perspective's private working state, or repair a frozen claim after its outcomes are visible.

#### Contrarian viewpoints within the existing challenger role

Clarification requested on October 31, 2026 (SESSION-2026-10-31-CONTRARIAN), within D086 / agent-inquiry/1.0. The user's original wording in the current repository conversation was:

> könnten wir an der passenden stelle im framework hinzufügen das auch contrarian viewpoints erlaubt sind, umd die thesen zu testen? Diese Contrarian Agenten dürften aber nicht jeden Müll behaupten, nur um dagegen zu halten

Contrarian viewpoints are permitted to probe a thesis, its assumptions and its proposed test. They can challenge either the candidate or a prevailing explanation. This clarifies the existing challenger responsibility; it does not require an additional agent or a debate for every candidate.

- Address the actual claim and scope faithfully. Identify the assumption, mechanism, measurement or inference being challenged; do not replace it with an easier claim to attack.
- Distinguish a sourced observation, a logical counterexample under stated assumptions, and an untested alternative explanation. Cite the permitted source or expose the premises and uncertainty. Do not invent facts, citations, measurements or numerical effects, or present speculation as an observed refutation.
- Explain how the objection could matter and what permitted observation or check could distinguish the alternatives. A plausible but untested counterhypothesis may be retained as a proposal; unavailable evidence remains an explicit gap. Neither a novel idea nor a rival explanation must already have empirical support to be considered.
- Prefer consequential, checkable objections over generic doubt. There is no objection quota, reward for disagreement, or requirement to defend a position contradicted by the available evidence. Correct or withdraw an objection when its basis fails; retain that disposition in the existing record. "No substantiated objection found" remains acceptable and supplies no scientific support.

Use the existing inquiry summary to retain the challenged claim, basis, proposed check and disposition without a new score or mandatory form. Apply the same evidentiary discipline to supportive and opposing arguments. An unusual claim cannot be rejected merely because a challenger finds it implausible (REJ-046); surviving criticism cannot establish scientific validity (REJ-037). Existing independent review and deterministic evaluation retain their respective authority. Access, perspective isolation, budgets, exposure and freeze boundaries above still apply. Apply this clarification through the next applicable versioned prompt/review configuration under D026; do not silently change an active epoch or reopen a frozen claim.

### 4. Record a short investigation summary

At completed investigations, handoffs and justified continuation decisions, retain the following in the existing Journal or review artifact:

> Purpose -> finding with source -> warranted conclusion -> remaining uncertainty -> next action with reason.

This is a concise artifact summary, not a transcript of private reasoning or a form before every tool call. An existing record can satisfy it; do not create duplicate reports. Record findings separately from interpretations. Where no finding is available, say so. Finding a measurement limit, rejecting an assumption or obtaining no viable hypothesis are admissible investigation summaries, without automatically establishing framework success.

Review exploration at those work boundaries using the existing resource limits and continuation rules. No new universal timer or automatic clarification-value score is introduced. Resource exhaustion is not market falsification. A later restart retains the prior purpose, findings, uncertainty and exposure rather than disguising repeated work as a fresh investigation.

## Optional subagent delegation

Source: SESSION-2026-10-31-SUBAGENTS, the current repository conversation. The user explicitly permitted framework subagents:

> Unser Framework darf auch subagenten spanen

The framework may spawn subagents when delegation serves a concrete task, including grounded contrarian review, formalization, independent checks or separable technical work. This is optional execution of existing responsibilities under D030/D079/D086, not a compulsory many-agent architecture. The permission is not limited to contrarian work and does not require asking the user again for each ordinary delegation within an authorized task.

Following the user's October 31 refinement in the same conversation, use subagents selectively under central coordination. Delegate when a specific separable task, independent check or context boundary justifies the overhead; do not spawn agents simply because the capability is available. The owning controller retains responsibility for task allocation, integration and completion. Recursive spawning is not the default: a child returns any further delegation need to that controller, which decides whether the additional bounded task is justified within the existing authority and allowance. This requires no routine user approval and is not a blanket prohibition on justified deeper delegation.

Do not create a separate evaluation project, agent tournament or new prerequisite workstream for this permission. Use the checks and evidence already required for the selected task to assess returned work; record concrete failures, duplication or overhead in existing records when relevant. Permission to delegate is not evidence of improved quality, speed or cost.

The owning controller assigns a bounded task, the applicable role/profile, permitted input artifacts, expected output and a share of the existing resource allowance. Reuse the existing queue, worker identities, scoped artifacts and completion checks. Where a spawn/dispatch path is not implemented, retain that implementation gap rather than claiming that permission establishes capability.

All descendant calls, retries, tokens, costs and elapsed work remain subject to the applicable parent task/epoch accounting and stop conditions. Delegation cannot create a fresh allowance or escape cancellation, revocation or an exhausted cap. Child access is limited to the delegated role and authorized task; a child cannot acquire broader rights merely by spawning another child. Preserve ancestry, input/output provenance, failures and inherited exposure. The controller checks returned evidence before accepting completion; a subagent's success assertion alone is insufficient.

Subagents remain subject to the same grounded-inquiry rule. A distinct process, name or model does not establish scientific independence or confer verdict authority. Preserve role separation and inaccessible peer working state. Parallel execution is permitted only where the active contract/profile allows it; the initial two isolated, sequential discovery perspectives and their shared budget remain unchanged. Any material change to that research design or a frozen prompt/tool profile still follows D008/D026. This documents permission and integration requirements, not a verified general autonomous spawning capability.

## Operational monitoring of delegated work

Source: SESSION-2026-10-31-SUBAGENT-MONITORING, the current repository conversation. After reviewing research and the existing accounting/feedback paths, the user approved the proposed lightweight monitoring with "machen wir so". Scope: per-task quality and complete resource accounting, separate outcome categories, task-specific verification and a compact section in existing reports. No separate evaluation project, agent tournament, universal quality score or automatic scientific/prompt change is authorized by this approval.

Reuse the existing controller records, provider usage ledger and [feedback observation contract](13_GENERATOR_FEEDBACK_AND_EVALUATION.md#minimum-retained-information). These are required record meanings, not new lifecycle states or a parallel database:

| Dimension | Retained information |
|---|---|
| Assignment | Purpose, parent/job identity, role, available model/prompt/profile versions, permitted inputs and expected observable output. Unknown model revisions remain unknown. |
| Outcome | Verified completion, partial completion, a justified gap, failure or pending/unverified work, mapped to existing statuses; link the actual output and check rather than accepting a success assertion. |
| Quality | Applicable check results, confirmed defects, unsupported assertions, later-discovered defects and correction/withdrawal, with scope and reviewer/check provenance. |
| Resources | Calls, input/output tokens, cost basis, outstanding reservations, elapsed time, retries, checking and attributable rework; preserve failed and interrupted attempts. |
| Coordination | Observed duplicated work, missing handoff information and unnecessary delegation, with supporting records rather than inferred blame. |

The owning controller applies existing deterministic checks where suitable, such as arithmetic/replay, access, output structure and source identity. A valid link or schema alone does not establish that a source supports a claim or that a formalization preserves meaning. Reuse the applicable independent semantic review for those questions; model judgments remain qualified assessments, not scientific evidence or proof of independent thought. Investigate concrete anomalies and include a small, budgeted sample of otherwise ordinary outputs to look for missed defects. Declare that sample's selection and resource cap before inspection in the existing work record, preserve its coverage/limits, and stay within current data and spending permissions. Do not add a mandatory evaluator call to every output or require a new study before continuing unrelated authorized work.

For contrarian work, retain confirmed objections, refuted/withdrawn objections, plausible but untested alternatives, and no substantiated objection as distinct descriptions. Objection count is not a reward. A qualified absence of objections or a correctly identified capability gap can be a valid task outcome; neither establishes a market finding. Confirmed objections alone cannot estimate missed-error frequency. Any sampled quality summary must retain its denominator, unchecked cases and sampling limitations.

Account for the entire delegated branch, including descendants, retries, verification and attributable rework. Count each underlying call/cost once; do not add an already-inclusive parent total to its child totals. Keep shared or unallocated overhead visible rather than inventing precise attribution. Distinguish provider-reported token usage, tariff-derived estimates or conservative bounds, unresolved reservations and invoice amounts where available. Missing usage is not zero, and a reservation is not a settled charge. Separate branch wall-clock duration from summed worker time when work overlaps. Include the cost of monitoring itself in the existing allowance; delegation and checks do not replenish it.

Add only a compact section to the existing report: counts and outcomes by comparable role/configuration/task group, resource totals and accounting coverage, verification coverage, rework and actionable findings. Retain pending, failed and gap cases. Do not rank agents from a few heterogeneous tasks, collapse the dimensions into a scalar score, or reward positive market results. Existing budget/access/stop rules remain enforced by the controller. Quality concerns trigger a bounded diagnosis through the current workflow; they do not automatically select models, rewrite prompts or change scientific statuses. Passive operational monitoring does not establish the causal benefit of delegation over a non-delegated alternative or general reliability.

Research basis: [AI Agents That Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502) motivates examining quality with cost; [MAST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657) motivates tracing design, coordination and verification failures; [tau-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12045) distinguishes achieved outcomes and repeated-run reliability; [Judging the Judges](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07791) documents evaluator bias. These sources motivate the design, not universal thresholds or demonstrated benefits in this framework. Existing [provider usage documentation](../../trading_researcher/OPENAI.md#persistent-consumption-documentation) describes accounting building blocks; full descendant monitoring and the behavioral cases below remain unverified until implemented and exercised.

## Acceptance examples for TEST-14

These extend the future behavioral acceptance specification for REQ-014; they are not executed agent tests.

| Situation | Required observable result |
|---|---|
| A reviewer is asked about a framework gap but receives a general essay | The reviewer distinguishes the essay from the affected framework artifacts and limits any conclusion to the inspected object. |
| A targeted data query investigates a possible measurement artifact | Its purpose identifies the uncertainty and how different findings could change interpretation; measurement validity is allowed as indirect clarification. |
| Discovery begins without a hypothesis or rival explanation | It can proceed with a stated exploration purpose within permitted resources; absence of a finished question is not a rejection gate. |
| Exploration changes direction or produces no viable hypothesis | The trajectory retains the changed purpose and the summary reports the actual finding or lack of one, uncertainty and reason for the next action. |
| A challenger offers an untested rival mechanism | It states the challenged assumption, explicit premises and uncertainty, and a discriminating permitted check; it retains the alternative as a proposal rather than an observed refutation. |
| A challenger invents a citation or asserts an unmeasured effect | The assertion cannot be used as an established finding; verification, correction or withdrawal and the remaining uncertainty are retained in the existing record. |
| An unusual candidate is dismissed solely as implausible, or a counterargument fails | Plausibility alone grants no rejection authority; an unsupported objection is corrected or withdrawn without forcing a replacement objection or treating the candidate as scientifically supported. |
| The challenger finds no supported objection | It reports that result without inventing criticism or treating absence of objections as scientific support. |
| Formalization changes the question's scope or outcome | The existing semantic-difference/gap path exposes the discrepancy instead of silently substituting a different claim. |
| An agent delegates a bounded check to a subagent | The owning controller identifies the concrete delegation purpose, retains task/role/input lineage and checks the returned evidence; all child usage is accounted within the applicable parent allowance. Any further delegation need returns to that controller rather than triggering recursive spawning by default. |
| A child requests broader access, a fresh budget or delegation after a stop | Existing access, accounting and stop controls apply across descendants; a child identity cannot bypass the boundary. |
| A delegated branch includes retries, checking, shared costs and an unresolved receipt | Each underlying cost is counted once, monitoring overhead and incomplete accounting remain visible, and reservations are separate from settled charges; no fresh allowance follows. |
| A monitoring report summarizes contrarian outcomes or sampled quality | It preserves distinct dispositions, denominators, unchecked cases and sampling limits without rewarding objection counts or claiming scientific support or proven delegation benefit. |
| A completed investigation is handed off or continued | The existing artifact carries the concise summary and provenance; no per-call duplicate form is required. |
| A budget cap or unavailable measurement ends work | A resource decision or typed gap is retained separately from a scientific negative; restart preserves exposure. |
| An agent invokes clarification value to request protected outcomes or change frozen criteria | Existing access/freeze controls deny the operation; the working rule grants no override. |
