## Stable potential-event identity

Within the adopted scope an identity contains exactly the versioned event schema
and these coordinates:

- Market domain `binance-spot`; instrument `BTCUSDT` or `ETHUSDT`.
- Grid size 900 seconds.
- Decision time and outcome anchor as nonnegative UTC integer seconds, both
  aligned to that grid. The anchor is strictly later than the decision.

Snapshot, revision, compiler/program, claim and partition names are provenance,
not another event identity. Different transformations of the same event cannot
manufacture unused evidence. Retain the complete original claim and exact
source/value versions separately. A correction appends provenance under the
same event identity; it neither overwrites an earlier value nor resets prior use.
An unknown market namespace or an unqualified mapping to this scope remains a
gap. Other domains require their own applicable definition, not silent coercion.

## Potential grid, observed outcomes and input footprints

Define the potential grid from owned immutable compilation metadata before
outcome inspection. Decision positions use the declared aligned half-open
universe `[start,end)`; `anchor = decision + horizon_bars * 900` must be strictly
before `end` to belong to its potential evaluable grid. Retain structurally
excluded boundary positions separately. Scope, missingness, pending publication
and observed outcome values do not create or change potential identities.

Potential membership does not assert that a row exists, its scope is true, its
outcome is eligible/completed, or its source is scientifically admissible. Actual
execution retains each such disposition under its original expression contract.
Different case counts are not additional independent information.

Keep the following separate:

1. Potential event coordinates and their exact source bindings.
2. Declared conservative raw-input footprints, including the applicable
   profile's complete nested lag/window requirements.
3. Actual inspected inputs, availability and label-information endpoints.
4. The independently qualified dependence, exposure and prior-use assessment.

Represent raw observation grid spans as
`[first_required_timestamp,last_required_timestamp + 900)`, including the last
required grid position. Retain exact actual label-information endpoints; a
separate diagnostic view is explicitly closed at decision and completion/
publication endpoints. An interval intersection is not an identity verdict or
a scientific dependence model. Unknown actual endpoints remain unknown.

Reuse the retained compiler and each expression profile's own lookback/input
rules. The legacy `outcomes._lookback` alone does not cover exact-composition
median/product/Boolean windows. Unsupported or uncertain membership remains a
gap. This contract does not replace those existing numerical, availability,
warmup or required-input meanings.

same-event intersection with another allocated batch cannot be reset by another
snapshot, revision, claim, key, source alias or restore. Exact duplicate requests

TRUE_FORWARD upgrade follows. Corroboration still requires its pre-reservation,

attempt accounting, revocation and restore owners. Caller declarations and
generic source schemas cannot issue a qualified reservation or scientific
verdict. Intended use rechecks exact source/input/dependency currency. No partial
allocation or result is released after an interrupted or failed commitment.
