# Dave Mabe's Columns Approach: Research and Framework Fit

Research date: October 22, 2026. Repository basis: `codex/architecture`, commit `395aa375dd645c3191726f0b27a1da079d1e5904`; working tree clean at the beginning of this review.

Status: sourced research and nonbinding integration recommendations. This document changes no accepted decision, scientific contract, canonical definition, roadmap priority or implementation. Its success criterion is an attributable account of Mabe's workflow, its methodological relatives and limitations, and concrete compatibility analysis against the current specification. It is not a market experiment, product audit or whole-framework efficacy verdict.

## Framework model used for this assessment

The framework aims to let an LLM invent nontrivial market hypotheses freely, formalize them faithfully and have independent code evaluate them. It also aims to accelerate discovery and testing substantially and ultimately produce profitably usable hypotheses after realistic costs. These are distinct from proving superiority over another generator. The governing sources are the [Problem Definition](../architecture/01_PROBLEM_DEFINITION.md), [Master Architecture](../architecture/06_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md), D001/D068-D070 in the [Decision Register](../architecture/02_DECISION_REGISTER.md), and the [Generator Feedback and Evaluation Contract](../architecture/13_GENERATOR_FEEDBACK_AND_EVALUATION.md).

An ordinary research path begins with permitted point-in-time data and a frozen knowledge/experience snapshot. Discovery forms a question and may make recorded deterministic queries. It registers H_idea; formalization produces H_exec using defined, certified measurements. The evaluator's plan, selection/exposure checks and dependencies must be settled before the atomic freeze. Execution returns cases and paths; independent code assigns qualified evidence under that plan. A policy constructed from the resulting knowledge has a separate freeze and evaluation. A useful column may help discovery, formalization and diagnosis along this path, but does not grant evidence authority.

A failure path matters equally: an idea requiring an unavailable measurement remains retained with its capability gap and retry condition. A technical error quarantines the affected run rather than falsifying the market claim. Abandonment, gaps, errors, valid negative findings and inconclusive outcomes enter the Research Journal. Research Experience may derive qualified heuristics for later epochs; the Market Model retains qualified evidence, including negative/conflicting evidence. Policy Experience remains separate, and operations cannot directly rewrite research truth. D070 requires observations, interpretations and later uses to be recorded distinctly, with inherited exposure.

Logical components can share an application. CMP-01 owns permitted data/context, CMP-03 feature specification, CMP-04 certification references/compilation/freeze, CMP-05 execution, CMP-06 evidence, CMP-07 experience, CMP-09 policies and CMP-12 material-change governance. Independent certification is stronger than the feature author approving its own implementation. Material scientific changes need explicit approval; the routine-certification boundary remains OPEN-14.

The repository is documentation-only, including its Column Library. The [Current State](../architecture/CURRENT_STATE.md) records no runtime or executed market experiment. OPEN-01/04 concern lifecycle and executable semantics; OPEN-05/06/12 concern statistics, data/execution and confirmation allocation; OPEN-11 concerns success/assessment thresholds. OPEN-02 still blocks affected claims of automatic subgroup evidence transport. This report does not resolve them. The feasibility experiment remains the highest-priority future empirical milestone, deferred for missing data under the [Roadmap](../architecture/10_IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md).

The relevant safeguard already exists: D044-D047, DEF-032 and REQ-026 distinguish reusable measurements, their time classes and exploratory selection. Even perfect implementation would not establish valuable discoveries, adequate effective sample size, model-unseen history or profitable policies. Those remain empirical questions.

## What Mabe actually proposes

In his May 11, 2023 article, Mabe adds a filter-analysis phase to a trade-list backtest. Instead of repeatedly recoding a guessed relative-volume threshold, retain the original trades and append the measured relative volume. Analyze candidate restrictions on that richer table before deciding which to implement. This separates expensive simulation from relatively cheap exploration. His reported speed and profitability improvements are practitioner claims, not independently verified results in this review. This is the earliest direct explanation located here, not a verified invention date. [M1](https://davemabe.com/youre-probably-backtesting-wrong)

His May 16, 2025 article explicitly connects the idea to software reuse: implement a measurement in a shared library so later backtests can use it. This supports attributing the library analogy to Mabe himself. It does not establish that he invented reusable features or originated the related statistical methods. [M2](https://davemabe.com/your-library-of-columns)

The public Strategy Cruncher documentation shows a CSV-driven workflow with ranked filter suggestions, comparison curves and trade/performance metrics. It tells users to think about suggestions, choose at most one, apply it and submit another backtest. That is documented iterative selection. The inspected pages do not expose enough of the algorithm to identify its exact estimator, search space or error-control mechanism. It should not be described as a particular regression, tree algorithm or formal statistical test without further evidence. [M3](https://app.davemabe.com/docs/welcome-step-one), [M4](https://app.davemabe.com/docs/welcome-step-two)

His February 2026 materials add continuous measurements, normalization and intra-trade comparisons. In the podcast, distance from a moving average is contrasted with a binary above/below flag. Mabe explicitly distinguishes entry-known measurements from later information: values recorded at exit can still represent information saved at entry; later profit observations may compare exits but cannot justify the earlier entry. The ATR-normalized MACD discussion is introduced by co-host Michael Nauss, not presented as Mabe's own indicator invention. [M5](https://davemabe.com/how-to-build-your-column-library), [M6](https://lineyourownpockets.com/episodes/how-to-grow-your-column-library/transcript)

For illustration only, a research table could contain an eligible event ID, decision time, relative volume and distance from a reference price, followed by separately permissioned future-return outcomes. A hypothetical question is whether outcomes differ with distance or volatility. No particular threshold or positive relationship is asserted. A column is a measurement representation; the empirical hypothesis is a relationship involving measurements and outcomes. A filter is an action or sample-selection rule. These three objects should not be conflated.

Mabe's practical contribution is therefore the packaging of measurement reuse and rapid trade-level exploration into a trader's workflow. The statistical core belongs to established exploratory analysis, conditional-response modeling and rule/subgroup search. The library itself does not require machine learning; arithmetic, plots and grouped comparisons can implement much of the exploratory work.

## Benefits, risks and what the evidence supports

The plausible engineering benefit is amortization: compute a well-defined measurement once per relevant data/version/time context and reuse it across permitted queries. This can reduce repeated simulation, coding and inconsistent definitions. The research benefit is greater visibility: aggregate PnL hides conditional patterns, tails, missingness, interactions and failure paths. Retaining numerical values can postpone premature threshold choices, while normalized measures can make selected comparisons more interpretable. These are reasoned advantages; their magnitude in this framework is unmeasured.

There is also a productive feedback opportunity. A surprising column/outcome relationship can lead the LLM to a new question, a negative control, a competing explanation or a missing measurement. A technically correct column that is unhelpful for one hypothesis may remain useful elsewhere. Conversely, "this feature looked promising before" is a qualified search heuristic, not transferable market evidence. D070 already supplies the appropriate observation/interpretation/use separation.

**Adaptive selection is the primary scientific danger.** Trying many columns, cutoffs, time periods, transformations, directions, horizons and combinations can produce an attractive survivor under noise. A one-rule final strategy may encode a huge search. Changing only one filter per iteration does not erase earlier comparisons. A ledger makes that search observable but cannot, by itself, correct its statistical consequences. White, Cawley-Talbot and Dwork provide different formal treatments of this family of problems; none supplies an automatic universal correction for an open-ended LLM search. [A6](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~behansen/718/White2000.pdf), [A7](https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html), [A8](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Paper.pdf)

Mabe does discuss overfitting and out-of-sample performance. However, on April 17, 2025 he emphasizes increasing trade counts, and on July 8, 2025 he argues that with sufficiently strong signals and enough trades, the in-sample/out-of-sample distinction will not matter because optimal suggestions coincide. Those are genuine points of disagreement with this framework. More observations may help, but common shocks and overlapping trades limit independent information. Similar fitted thresholds do not prove an untouched evaluation. If outcomes influenced selecting that threshold, they cannot independently validate its descendant. The strength of the signal is itself unknown when discovery begins. [M7](https://davemabe.com/the-easiest-way-to-avoid-curve-fitting), [M8](https://davemabe.com/out-of-sample-performance)

**Time leakage can enter through an innocent column name.** Final daily volume is unavailable during the morning. Final MAE/MFE and time-to-high are unknown before their measurement interval ends. Historical revisions may have early event timestamps but late availability. Fitting a normalizer or imputation rule on the full dataset also leaks information into earlier partitions. Mabe's entry/later distinction is useful, while the project's DEF-031/032/033 require more explicit availability, latency and version semantics. [Canonical Definitions](../architecture/04_DEFINITIONS.md)

**Filtering a saved trade list has an execution boundary.** Suppose trade A used the only available position slot and prevented trade B. Removing A from the exported list does not reconstruct B, its fill or its return. A new exit can alter buying power, subsequent signals, transaction costs, ranking and position size. Accordingly, table filtering can faithfully describe the retained original trades, but a new policy generally needs resimulation when those interactions matter. This is an engineering deduction, consistent with the existing [Scientific Evaluation](../architecture/07_SCIENTIFIC_EVALUATION.md) and [Blueprint](../architecture/09_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md), not an observed defect in Cruncher's code.

**Selection also determines what the table can say.** A list of executed trades cannot reveal opportunities already excluded by the original scanner, portfolio constraints or missing data. Broad event research should retain eligibility and exclusion reasons within a declared scope. It need not enumerate every market observation. The canonical scope baseline includes all eligible scope cases; a filtered subset must not silently become its own baseline. An aggregate effect does not automatically certify its subgroups, and a conditional association does not establish its causal explanation.

**A useful representation can become a restrictive ontology.** Only exposing existing indicator columns could turn free invention into a closed menu. Single-column rankings can miss interactions; correlated indicators can provide redundant views of one effect. Continuous values preserve some information, but genuine binary events and nonlinear or nonmonotonic relationships remain legitimate. Normalization introduces its own definitions and can remove a level effect that matters. REJ-042 explicitly rejects mandatory continuity, quintiles or monotonicity; REJ-001/045 reject replacing discovery with blind enumeration. [Rejected Decisions](../architecture/03_REJECTED_DECISIONS.md)

**Reuse propagates both benefits and defects.** Mabe's attractive software analogy of fixing a shared function requires a scientific qualification: a new version must not silently change a frozen historical experiment. New versions, affected-dependency review and retained old evidence are required. A cache should be sensitive to data snapshot, computation version, parameters and time/availability context. A growing library also creates storage, computation and maintenance costs; compute requested capabilities before assuming every conceivable column belongs in every run. [M2](https://davemabe.com/your-library-of-columns), [A9](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper/2015/file/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Paper.pdf)

Finally, improved conditional returns or win rates are not sufficient for economic usefulness. Retained opportunities, net costs, portfolio exposure, tail losses, capacity and uncertainty all matter. A sparse filter can improve average trade quality while reducing total usable opportunity. Nonstationarity remains possible even with honest historical evaluation. These are existing policy/economic requirements, not a reason to forbid exploratory filtering.

## Concrete combination with the existing framework

The main recommendation is to make the accepted Column Library operational through existing interfaces when a bounded implementation is authorized. Adding another discovery engine, evidence engine or feature-store service is unnecessary for this purpose.

| Use | Existing architectural home | Smallest coherent application | Boundary / relevant future acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reusable measurement definitions | CMP-01/03, FeatureDefinition, IF-04; D004/D044 | Specify units, inputs, parameters, missingness, availability and independent fixtures; register a version | Certification establishes computation, not predictive value; REQ-003 / TEST-03 |
| Entry, evolving and completed-path data | CMP-05 outputs and CMP-01 permissioned views; D045, DEF-032 | Keep event/trade identity and decision time; expose each class only for its permitted purpose | No completed-path values as earlier inputs; REQ-026 / TEST-26 |
| Exploratory questions and diagnostics | CMP-02 queries via IF-02; D016/D046/D051 | Ask a bounded question, compute the requested distributions/interactions and retain the query and exposure | No catalog-only discovery or unrecorded exhaustive search; REQ-014/024 |
| Outcome-driven filter proposal | H_idea/H_exec, CMP-04/06; D031/D046/D049 | Register alternatives and selected relationship, then freeze the applicable evaluation contract | Historical exploration does not become fresh confirmation; OPEN-05/12 |
| Exit/management/sizing research | CMP-09, PolicySpec and IF-10; D017/D048/D050 | Compare path diagnostics, then evaluate the selected full policy under its own execution assumptions | No evidence inheritance from components; resimulate when interaction changes; TEST-09/12/26 |
| Reusable research lessons | CMP-07 and D070, IF-08 | Store exploratory observation, interpretation, counterevidence and subsequent use separately | Exposure follows the lesson; no automatic market-knowledge upgrade; TEST-28 |
| Faster iteration | CMP-11 diagnostics; D068/D070 | Measure end-to-end discovery/testing time and cost for a fixed information/quality scope | Faster arithmetic is not demonstrated faster useful discovery; OPEN-11 / TEST-27/29 |

Three data views are a useful implementation sketch, not adopted schemas: an event/entry view, a decision-time state view and a completed-outcome/path view. A common store may serve them with different permissions. Storage co-location does not authorize discovery access to protected evaluation outcomes. A column's definition, computed value and empirical usefulness remain separate artifacts or separately represented facts.

For an illustrative 15-minute-or-higher research path, the LLM could ask whether recovery after a specified excursion depends jointly on pre-event volatility and distance from a reference level. The observation itself is not a mandatory template. The library supplies those measurements if certified; otherwise the original idea receives a retained gap. A permitted exploratory query suggests a boundary. The resulting H_exec must still define the scope, outcome, horizon, comparison and effect criterion; the selected boundary is marked as fitted where appropriate. It inherits the query's exposure and receives separate confirmation allocation. If an exit or sizing policy is subsequently proposed, it follows its own trial path. No market, threshold, estimator or effect direction is selected by this example.

The most useful next preparation, if requested, would be a small measurement-contract example and data-free acceptance fixtures tied to the already-prioritized feasibility slice. Candidate fixtures are: an unavailable late revision; full-path MFE offered at entry; a known binary event; a nonlinear interaction; a missing primitive; a library-version change; a capital-constrained filter requiring resimulation; and a heuristic derived from an old holdout attempting to reuse that holdout as fresh evidence. These are proposed future checks, not executed tests. They should be chosen for the pilot's actual requirements rather than turned into a new prerequisite platform.

Existing safeguards should be preserved, not merely restated as new proposals. Additional field choices, cache design and simulator capability checks are engineering detail. Scientific policy choices remain with their open decisions, and routine autonomous certification must not be invented around OPEN-14. There is no recommendation here to mandate a broad column library, forbid other discovery routes, change evidence standards, build D015's rejected comparator generator or import MabeKit as the scientific authority.
