## Reconstructed system and review coverage

The [Problem Definition](../architecture/01_PROBLEM_DEFINITION.md) sets three related aims: free, nontrivial LLM invention with faithful independent evaluation; substantial acceleration of discovery and testing; and profitably usable hypotheses after realistic costs. Framework success differs from a supported individual claim and from a useful policy. D015 excludes a competing rule-catalog generator as a required baseline; it does not exclude statistical controls. Faster valid rejection and retained capability gaps can be useful progress without proving framework success.

The [Master Architecture](../architecture/06_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md) supplies the ordinary path. CMP-01 builds permitted, point-in-time context with inherited exposure. CMP-02 asks questions and records queries, then submits H_idea. CMP-03 formalizes faithfully using certified measurements. CMP-04 binds the claim, assigned evaluation plan, dependency versions, exposure and allocation in a durable freeze. CMP-05 returns execution artifacts; CMP-06 alone assigns the qualified empirical outcome. The Journal retains the trajectory; the Market Model retains qualified evidence. Later epochs can retrieve that knowledge and separately labeled research heuristics. Policy construction is a second search with its own freeze and evaluation, not automatic validation of a combination of supported components.

An early-exit path is equally important. A proposed measurement may be unavailable or impossible to formalize. The original idea, gap and retry condition survive in the Journal; experience can help later search without creating a negative market result. A pre-freeze attack may instead cause abandonment. A technical error quarantines affected execution. A valid empirical negative follows its frozen decision rule and remains distinct from these paths. All can inform later permitted snapshots, carrying their ancestry. Operations feed Policy Experience and registered future research observations, never directly rewrite scientific evidence.

Authority is divided by function, not by model name or agent count. Discovery proposes; certification checks computations; execution calculates; evaluation applies a frozen scientific policy; CMP-12 and user governance control material changes. [Document 13](../architecture/13_GENERATOR_FEEDBACK_AND_EVALUATION.md) distinguishes feedback observations, interpretations, uses and later assessment. A separate process or deterministic implementation alone does not make data statistically independent.

The current project is documentation only. There is no runtime, enforced access boundary, implemented Column Library or executed TEST-01 through TEST-30. The [Open Decisions](../architecture/11_OPEN_DECISIONS.md) still block the affected operations: lifecycle semantics (OPEN-01), subgroup transport and family-rate interpretation (OPEN-02/03), concrete evaluation and evidence allocation (OPEN-05/12), data authority (OPEN-06), framework assessment thresholds (OPEN-11), and routine certification boundaries (OPEN-14). The data-dependent feasibility experiment remains deferred. These gaps do not prevent clarifying accepted requirements now.

The review covered README, AGENTS, REVIEWING and Current State; architecture documents 01 and 05-13; decision dispositions and affected rationale in 02-03; definitions and relevant preserved formulas in 04; the source audit; the existing research report; and the documentation checker. This was a whole-specification review, not a fresh extraction of every archived message or a dependency-code audit. The archive's unavailable uploads and incomplete original-source coverage remain unresolved. Primary literature and access limits are recorded in the [scientific supplement](../architecture/07_SCIENTIFIC_EVALUATION.md#overfitting-controls-across-the-research-process).

## Findings before revision

| Finding and trigger | Existing protection and remaining gap | Consequence and smallest remedy |
|---|---|---|
| OF-01: An implementer cross-validates the final rule after inspecting all data to select its columns or normalizer | D014/D031/D046 require exposure and full selection accounting, but the scientific review did not explicitly contrast candidate assessment with procedure assessment | Outer scores can inherit global selection. Explain both evaluation objects and the placement of fitting/selection; no mandatory nested-CV recipe. |
| OF-02: A rejected holdout result, rank or aggregate score guides the next candidate through a dashboard or memory | D070 already separates tuning from assessment and D014 propagates exposure. Interface descriptions and acceptance examples did not make coarse feedback or selection through diagnostics concrete | Hiding raw rows can leave an information channel. Map these uses to existing query, journal, context and assessment records, with future adversarial fixtures. |
| OF-03: A large trade table, shared confirmation batch or repeatedly inspected confidence interval is treated as adequate independent evidence | Scientific Evaluation and document 13 already discuss dependence, joint policies and stopping. Pilot prerequisites do not collect these choices into an actionable decision sequence | False precision or accidental universal rules. Add a scoped protocol worklist distinguishing sampling unit, outcome overlap, multiplicity, release and stopping; leave method/threshold choices open. |
| OF-04: Calibration or a robustness plot is used to repair the candidate and then cited as its final validation | D025/D035/D038 and D070 require selection-aware calibration and separate assessment. The specific boundary around calibration tuning and diagnostic selection was implicit | The evaluator itself can be tuned to a convenient benchmark. Explain development versus final assessment, selector symmetry and retained failures; do not prescribe one null world or estimator. |
| OF-05: Filtering recorded trades is presented as the return of a changed executable policy | D045/D048 and Scientific Evaluation already warn about path dependence; the blueprint lacked a concrete resource-interaction case | Removing a trade may free a slot for an unrecorded trade. Add the smallest resimulation/capability example and connect it to existing policy tests. |

These are specification-clarity and future verification gaps, not evidence of an existing defective runtime. The existing safeguards are substantial. If faithfully implemented they would still leave unknown model pretraining exposure, finite information, assumption violations and market change; documentation cannot establish their empirical control.
