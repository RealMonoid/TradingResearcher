## Chosen integration shape

Use one immutable study manifest and an operator-owned operation that advances the next fixed world. Reuse the registry's immutable artifacts, append-only controller events, transactions and retained-source helpers. Do not copy another complete computation controller or add a service/database merely because the study has another logical role.

Preparation binds:

- Exact owned plan, development execution and matching diagnostics, including source bytes, runtime, original case descriptors and inherited exposure.
- Every declared population stress specification, ordered eligible target grid, fixed repetitions per cell and available historical-window boundary. Targets count joint calendar positions and retain eligible/conditioned-count distributions; they are not independent observations.
- Every zero, delta and twice-delta scenario assigned by the plan, including both signs for a nondirectional claim. Keep the actual primary family count and marginal error budget visible.
- All world identities and generation/bootstrap seeds, with a purpose-specific development namespace. Retries use the original identities and both seeds; final calibration has a separate reserved namespace and fresh worlds.
- Actual fixed-count estimator and MC policy, explicit outer uncertainty settings, aggregate compute/storage limits, per-attempt reservations, bounded retry policy, fixed world order, treatment of every gap/failure and complete-grid candidate rule.
- A measured full-pipeline cost estimate and its workload qualification. Timing extrapolation does not guarantee the whole grid fits its cap.

A retained child performs `build_model -> generate_world -> stationary_bootstrap.calculate -> MC assessment` for one world. Another isolated child replays the same complete pipeline and compares retained outputs. Generated reference-shaped cases remain synthetic estimator inputs. They must not be relabeled as an owned `reference-execution/1` to bypass the existing bootstrap ownership checks.

## Durable accounting and recovery

Append the reservation and started-attempt identity atomically before spawning. Admit a new attempt only when settled charges plus outstanding full reservations plus the new reservation fit the frozen aggregate caps. Accepting the result, settling its resource charge and closing the attempt must commit together. Reject stale or already settled attempts.

Measure parent-observed elapsed work through generation, bootstrap, MC, replay, hashing and retention. Do not trust child-reported costs or reset the aggregate budget on RPC retry, process restart or a new request key. A retry adds cost while preserving the planned world and seeds. An uncertain interrupted attempt consumes its full reservation; no refund is inferred from wall-clock guesses.

Storage accounting must explicitly cover temporary, aborted and retained output, with a declared logical-byte versus physical-byte convention. Hash deduplication is not an undeclared budget discount. Retention caps need to include raw world/draw bytes or an independently checked lossless representation; missing output must not be replaced by a favorable summary.

The current generic child runner kills and reaps a child when the live parent catches cancellation/timeout. That does **not** prove child termination after an abrupt parent death. The single-writer registry lock establishes writer ownership, not process-tree termination. Before automatically retrying such an attempt, implement reliable containment/reclamation or verify the prior process identity and termination. Otherwise retain an explicit terminal technical/resource gap. Do not infer liveness or termination from a lock file, elapsed time or a persisted status label.

Existing bound-computation recovery can mark an interrupted writer, but that event alone cannot authorize launching replacement orphan work. This is an implementation prerequisite for a truthful restart/resume claim, not a request to relax scientific rules or disable antivirus protections.

## Complete study and candidate selection

Retain every planned world, population truth, resulting counts, interval/MC result, bytes and failure category. A fully evaluated information gap or unstable MC outcome is an abstention and contributes zero detections to its fixed operating-characteristic denominator. A timeout, crash, invalid result or missing output remains technically unresolved. It cannot be counted as a scientific negative, silently omitted or used to certify a low error rate.

Only a completely evaluated fixed grid may nominate the smallest **tested** target whose point detection rate reaches the adopted 80% design target at twice delta for every required stress specification and sign. Report zero and boundary behavior as well. Do not stop on a favorable crossing, assume monotone power, extrapolate an untested target or require an undeclared lower confidence bound of 80%.

Report this as a development information-target candidate conditional on the declared models, family and historical window. Preserve `n_min: null`, scientific readiness false and the missing adequacy, fresh calibration, data/semantic acceptance and atomic-freeze gates. A small engineering study is not the required 256-fresh-world calibration or full-process assessment.

## Exact outer uncertainty

For the proposed two-sided 95% pointwise Clopper-Pearson diagnostic, invert `P_p(X >= k) = 0.025` for the lower endpoint and `P_p(X <= k) = 0.025` for the upper endpoint. Use exact integer polynomial comparisons on a fixed dyadic grid, return outward bracket endpoints and retain the bracket width. Use lower zero at k=0 and upper one at k=n. This is a computation proposal for uncertainty reporting, not a changed admission threshold. [NIST's exact-limit definition](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/exacbici.htm) and [SciPy's documented exact method](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.16.1/reference/generated/scipy.stats._result_classes.BinomTestResult.proportion_ci.html) supply the reference basis.

Two separate one-sided 95% bounds do not form a two-sided 95% interval. Pointwise grid intervals do not become simultaneous or selection-adjusted after choosing a target. Their probability interpretation assumes independent outer worlds under the declared simulation law; the finite PRNG and fitted market model require separate qualifications.

Keep the approved calibration comparisons distinct: at 256 repetitions, the one-sided 95% upper error bound at most 0.10 permits at most 17 errors; the one-sided lower detection bound at least 0.70 requires at least 192 detections. The point design target of 80% instead requires 205 of 256, or 52 of 64. These are different questions. The existing planning assessment records the exact arithmetic derivation; no new estimator calibration follows.

1. A complete manifest and all seeds survive source changes, same-key conflict, backup/restore and interruption without replacement.
2. One genuine operator RPC advances the exact next world through generation, the prescribed estimator, MC and separate replay; arbitrary or protected source references fail before data access.
3. Concurrent advance, stale settlement, crash between reservation/spawn/commit, timeout, unknown usage and insufficient storage cannot reset caps or publish a favorable partial candidate.
4. Every planned world remains in the accounting. Statistical abstention and technical incompleteness have distinct denominators/statuses. Complete-grid selection and both nondirectional signs have independently fixed examples.
5. Exact binomial endpoint cases, outward brackets and the adopted one-sided calibration comparisons agree with independently computed fixtures.
6. A finite integration profile completes or stops honestly within its declared cap, retaining all results. Only after this boundary works should larger model-adequacy/power work proceed; fresh final calibration remains separately protected.
