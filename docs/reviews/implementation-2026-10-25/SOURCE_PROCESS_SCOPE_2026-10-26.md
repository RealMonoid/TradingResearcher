A separate current-source process reproduces the complete result and every generated case byte.

The warranted conclusion is limited: the estimator-only stress generator does not guarantee that particular original-process identity. Algebraically feasible marginal rates cannot by themselves make this a faithful model of the original hypothesis. Its declared synthetic stress purpose remains legitimate. The same conclusion does not forbid the approved stationary block bootstrap: concatenated bootstrap blocks are a different object from an asserted raw-process population law.

[Morris, White and Crowther (2019), section 3.2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6492164/), distinguishes simulation aims, data-generating mechanisms and estimands. Simulation findings apply to their scenarios; deliberately unrealistic stress models can serve a stated purpose, while ordinary empirical resampling does not manufacture a true null. This is methodological support for stating scope, not acceptance of a specific market law or universal numerical threshold.
