# Recorded bounded study continuation

Date: October 26, 2026. Scope: ordinary resource-control infrastructure under research-contract/1.0 section 9/D079 and existing technical revocation. The complete seven-package goal remains in progress.

## Purpose and authority

The existing runner stopped when the next complete frozen attempt would not fit its original aggregate compute/storage allowance. Section 9 permits additional computation only through a recorded continuation decision. The engineering question was whether that path could preserve original scientific commitments and every prior cost/failure without budget resets, replacement worlds or stale-source migration.

The implemented operator-only `continue_study` applies to scalar and family development/final series. It commits an immutable operations decision and authoritative event atomically, checks an exact chronological resource checkpoint, and adds at most 60 minutes compute and 4 GiB logical storage per explicit decision. At least one addition is positive; the addition must cover the next complete original attempt and 1 MiB decision metadata. There is no automatic renewal. The original manifest, numerical source, world/seed inventory, draws and retry caps remain unchanged.

Only an eligible resource stop can continue. Unknown use, orphan uncertainty, outstanding work, model gaps, exhausted retries, completion and any historical overrun prevent a grant. Every reservation/settlement prefix is checked: a temporary over-reservation cannot be hidden by a later lower settlement or grant. Two consecutive grants without newly completed world inventory exhaust the added operational no-progress fence. This does not define or certify scientific meaningful improvement under D030.

Exact request/key retries return the original grant without another charge. Reused checkpoints cannot stack grants. Attempts inherit the complete grant exposure and typed dependency. Final preparation authenticates the latest completed development attempts, checks their used grants, and retains those grant dependencies in final source/exposure and the typed revocation graph. Existing final assessment-source currency and protected ancestry remain enforced. Development results retain their ordinary release/exposure rules. The [runtime contract](../../../trading_researcher/INFORMATION_STUDIES.md#explicit-resource-continuation) documents the API and accounting convention.
