# System model for integration research

Date: October 24, 2026. Status: review reconstruction, not a new scientific contract.

## Investigation and evidence basis

The question is how to integrate the LLM researcher, four logical memories, and independent scientific evaluation into the existing framework. Recommendations must preserve autonomous nontrivial invention, faithful formalization, fast historical investigation, and economically useful research after realistic costs. A generic agent demonstration or a better task queue is insufficient.

[baseline.json](baseline.json) records its file hashes. This is not a claim about GitHub publication. Prior memory was used only for navigation; its older documentation-only status was superseded by current source inspection.

The entry documents, complete problem definition and master architecture, current contracts, affected decision/rejection bodies and definitions, requirements, scientific and engineering reviews, blueprint, roadmap and traceability were inspected. Long combined reads that truncated were followed by bounded reads of the affected sections. Historical source citations were used for authority navigation; the full original conversation was not reread. [The source audit](../../architecture/00_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) still records SRC-001 through SRC-008. Neither this review nor its recommendations recover missing images, source documents or acceptance messages.

The inquiry tests whether proposed integration mechanisms actually close missing behavior, whether they reuse existing infrastructure, and whether they introduce unauthorized scientific meaning, information access, or generator changes. A demonstrated existing implementation, a source contradiction, or a mechanism that cannot preserve these boundaries changes the recommendation.

## Goals and separate success levels

[D001, D068 and D069](../../architecture/02_DECISION_REGISTER.md) and the [problem definition](../../architecture/01_PROBLEM_DEFINITION.md) require free LLM invention, independent deterministic evaluation, substantial acceleration of discovery **and** testing, and eventual profitably usable hypotheses after realistic costs. Competitor superiority and a rule-based baseline generator are not required; D015 rejects that generator role. Statistical comparators and process-matched null controls remain necessary for their declared questions.

A candidate receives an outcome under a frozen scientific plan. A policy needs its own evidence, costs and risk criteria. Framework success additionally needs technical fidelity, appropriately scoped calibration, economic usefulness and measured acceleration. A negative candidate can be a valid and useful research result without establishing framework success. Synthetic correctness cannot establish market usefulness. Historical-first processing does not require a prolonged forward wait, and does not make exposed history unseen.

The initial contract is a bounded BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT spot, 15-minute, 2020-2025 profile, not a permanent restriction on ideas or information domains. Longer outcome horizons and swing research remain allowed. Live capital authority is zero. [Research-contract/1.0](../../architecture/14_RETAIL_RESEARCH_CONTRACT.md) governs the scoped details; old OPEN annotations in preserved reviews do not override later adoption.

## Complete research flow and authority

1. **CMP-01 / context:** resolve data versions, actual availability, allowed sources, certified measurements, memory snapshots and inherited exposure. Supply a frozen context with scoped tools. Bar timestamps alone do not establish point-in-time availability.
2. **CMP-02 / discovery:** two isolated, sequential perspectives use the same permitted base snapshot. They may find problems, ask deterministic measurement questions, research sources, propose counterexamples and invent free `H_idea` records. Their tool responses and subsequent information use become lineage. No quota forces a fixed number of ideas or mandatory discovery modes.
3. **CMP-03 / formalization:** preserve the original idea and explain semantic differences. Construct a typed executable expression only where meaning is clear. Missing data, features, expression semantics or estimators remain retained gaps; a convenient approximation cannot silently replace the question.
4. **CMP-04 / certification and freeze:** certify implementation against independently fixed semantics and cases; assign the scientific plan and family under code-owned policy; commit executable closure, trial identity and allocation atomically before assessment outcomes become accessible.
5. **CMP-05 / execution:** use an owned backend adapter on the authorized partition; return raw cases, paths, versions and validity checks. It cannot choose evidence status.
6. **CMP-06 / evidence:** verify the frozen manifest and valid results; apply the assigned estimator and decision rule; produce immutable evidence with uncertainty, origin and limits. Execution state, scientific outcome, usability, applicability, replication and capital authority remain separate.
7. **CMP-07/08 / retention and knowledge:** retain every branch. Release assessment information only under the frozen batch policy. Later knowledge/experience use inherits exposure, including summaries, ranks and pass/fail information.
8. **CMP-09 / policy:** construct rules with evidence, explicit assumptions or operational constraints; evaluate the combined policy separately through CMP-04/05/06. Subgroup membership and successful components do not transfer evidence automatically.
9. **CMP-10/11/12 / use, observation and improvement:** check applicability at intended use; retain incidents and pending work; propose material changes with independent assessment and approval. Operations cannot rewrite research evidence. No order interface follows from a research result.

Ownership and interfaces are defined in the [master architecture](../../architecture/06_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md) and [blueprint](../../architecture/09_IMPLEMENTATION_BLUEPRINT.md). These are logical responsibilities, not twelve services or agents. Open reasoning is agentic; fixed scientific operations remain deterministic (D034). A different model label is not proof of independence.

## Ordinary and failure paths

**Ordinary historical path:** a researcher notices a pattern using permitted development observations, records its queries and idea, and a formalizer expresses the actual claim using certified measurements. Code assigns the complete evaluation plan, reserves the assessment batch, and freezes it. The backend executes; the evaluator returns supported, negative or inconclusive under the plan. All outcomes and selection history survive. Once the batch is released, a later researcher may use that result, but its descendant inherits the result's exposure and cannot call the same observations independent confirmation. A derived trading policy receives its own trial and cost assessment.

**Early exit:** an idea requires a measurement or estimator that does not exist. Retain `H_idea`, the exact missing capability, provenance and retry condition; do not rename it to a simpler claim or call it false. Later certified capability can reopen formalization. A concrete pre-freeze counterexample may instead justify abandonment, also retained without a scientific negative.

**Technical failure and resource stop:** a corrupt dataset, inconsistent manifest or failed backend quarantines the attempt; it supplies no market verdict. Retry remains attached to the same frozen trial. A depleted budget can stop an inconclusive investigation; it cannot release only favorable partial results or manufacture falsification. A correction or rollback preserves prior attempts and exposure.

## Four feedback loops and four memories

| Loop | Storage and permitted influence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Market learning | Qualified EvidenceRecords enter the Market Model and later allowed contexts | Preserve uncertainty, scope, conflicts, dependencies and exposure; no truth claim from narrative confidence |
| Research experience | Journal trajectories, abandoned ideas, gaps, errors and outcomes support separately labeled heuristic interpretations | Record observation, interpretation, retrieval/use and review separately; failures and counterevidence remain retrievable |
| Policy experience | Separate policy tests, costs, fills and operational incidents guide policy research and registered later research questions | No direct rewrite of claims, evidence or protocols after losses |
| Material improvement | A diagnosed issue produces a versioned proposal, development probes, independent assessment and approved later activation | Tuning feedback is not final validation; rollback restores configuration, not freshness |

The [generator feedback contract](../../architecture/13_GENERATOR_FEEDBACK_AND_EVALUATION.md) specifies these distinctions. Research Journal, Market Model, Research Experience and Policy Experience are four logical views with different authority, not a requirement for four databases. More retained memory or fewer repeated errors does not establish better market discovery.

## Monitoring and reporting are part of the integration

[D087](../../architecture/16_TEMPORAL_DIAGNOSTIC_INVOCATION.md) binds a diagnostic plan before monitored outcomes. An eligible **completed-outcome** batch triggers a due check; a new bar with an unfinished horizon does not. Intended use also checks scope and freshness. Missing or uncertified methods remain visible. No quantitative monitoring method, universal cadence or live response is selected by the invocation contract.

[D088](../../architecture/17_MONITORING_VISIBILITY_AND_REPORTING.md) requires authorized method comparisons to start when ready, durable pending work, a weekly status report (default Monday 09:00 Europe/Berlin), and prompt actionable notices. A weekly reporting tick is not an extra scientific look. An LLM may render records but cannot activate a scientific method. Delivery credentials and quantitative method profiles remain separate inputs.

## Remaining inputs and limits

The [current disposition register](../../architecture/11_OPEN_DECISIONS.md) distinguishes resolved design from absent implementations and run-specific inputs. Outstanding prerequisites include exact model/prompt versions, a paid-run currency cap if needed, actual research data and availability/rights, applicable costs, precise dependence/randomization/information inputs, independently validated primitives and estimators, scientific freeze/allocation, calibrated inference and complete memory policies. The fixed numerical pilot choices already adopted in document 14 must not be silently replaced by vendor recommendations or paper defaults.

The local OS account and installed Python handlers are trusted. The queue's access checks do not constrain a worker with unrestricted shell/filesystem access. Provider adapters must expose only the scoped tool surface and keep evaluator storage inaccessible. The minimal adapter decision (D077) leaves Hermes optional, and vectorbt remains the recorded primary backend candidate; AmiBroker is an additional authorized option, not a silent replacement.

## Warranted conclusion before external research

The missing work is the connection and implementation of already-owned scientific and agent responsibilities, not the invention of a new all-powerful central "Brain." Existing infrastructure is reusable, but its technical completion and storage checks cannot stand in for research semantics. The literature review should discriminate between a bounded provider loop, general agent frameworks, automatic memory systems, and explicit scientific modules, then recommend an integration with testable boundaries. No claim of global architectural optimality, empirical efficacy, complete original-source recovery, or implemented scientific capability follows from this reconstruction.
