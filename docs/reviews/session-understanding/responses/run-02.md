The criticism is partly right, but its conclusion uses the wrong definition of useful progress. The project's objective is to accelerate both free hypothesis discovery and independent testing while preserving scientific decision quality—not merely to accumulate confirmed strategies.

What is right: convincing evidence for a modest strategy edge can require substantial independent information. “Sharpe 1” alone does not determine the necessary years: the estimand, uncertainty/power target, dependence, selection history, costs and stability assumptions matter. More 15-minute observations or instruments do not automatically create independent information. Exploratory memory cannot confer empirical support, erase inherited exposure or turn known history into a clean holdout (D014, D019; DEF-012, DEF-024–025). Fresh forward outcomes retain a distinct role in clean confirmation (D023), and even forward data need valid stopping and selection controls.

What is wrong: the inference from slow confirmation to an idle research loop. D013 and REJ-012 explicitly establish historical-first research while retaining forward confirmation. Existing years of observations can be processed without waiting those years again. The specified loop permits bounded questions, pre-freeze counterexample and feasibility checks, faithful formalization, frozen historical execution, independent evaluation, retained outcomes and qualified experience for the next epoch. These can improve search and eliminate weak candidates before stronger confirmation. A surviving critique is not validation; a technical failure or capability gap is not falsification; non-significance can be inconclusive. Historical results remain qualified by exposure and admissibility. A useful market finding also does not establish a profitable policy, whose synthesis and evaluation are separate.

What must be measured: predefine a comparison protocol, budgets and decision-quality criteria, then measure discovery latency and testing latency separately, end-to-end time to justified decisions, monetary/compute costs, bottlenecks and confirmation waiting time. Track outcome denominators, technical failures, inconclusive cases and retained gaps; neither rejection nor confirmation counts alone measure success. Evaluate the complete adaptive process on null worlds, planted effects and selection-matched placebos: false support, discovery power, effect/scope recovery and uncertainty. Test whether experience improves subsequent research under inherited-exposure constraints. Separately assess policy value after realistic costs and against appropriate alternatives.

These are evaluation needs, not new architectural decisions. OPEN-05, OPEN-11 and OPEN-12 leave statistical thresholds, framework success/stop criteria and confirmation allocation unresolved. The repository contains architecture only: acceleration, calibration and economic value are unverified. The criticism does not prove impossibility, and the design does not yet prove usefulness.
