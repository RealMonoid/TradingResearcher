The speed objective is project-consistent; the proposed shortcuts are not. The framework should accelerate both discovery and testing while preserving decision quality. This proposal affects the feedback-to-discovery loop and the formalization/confirmation boundary: historical feedback can guide future search, but cannot manufacture independent confirmation.

The proposal crosses four boundaries:

- **Exposure and independence:** After a holdout result informs a revision, that result belongs to the descendant's inherited exposure. Repeatedly rerunning the same holdout is adaptive historical search. Logging makes this auditable; it does not restore freshness or justify “independently confirmed” (D014, D019; REJ-014; DEF-014, DEF-025).
- **Frozen claims and selection:** Outcome-dependent rewriting creates a child hypothesis with preserved ancestry, a new trial identity and a new freeze—not a repair of the original frozen claim. “Until it succeeds” introduces outcome-dependent selection and stopping. The evaluation, family/selection and stopping policies must be fixed beforehand; independent code assigns evidence status. A sequential policy is not permission to rewrite claims opportunistically (D006, D031, D036; DEF-022).
- **Failure interpretation:** First distinguish a valid negative result from an inconclusive result, technical error or unavailable capability. Only the frozen evaluation criteria establish a negative result; technical failure provides neither support nor falsification. Preserve all outcomes and attempts (D007; DEF-017–020).
- **Free invention:** Discarding ideas outside the schema lets compiler limitations redefine discovery. Preserve H_idea, its DATA_GAP/LANGUAGE_GAP/evaluation gap and retry conditions. Bound current execution without imposing a closed hypothesis catalog (D001–D004). New primitives require independent certification; OPEN-14 does not yet define an automated approval boundary.

A consistent faster loop uses bounded questions, deterministic queries, feasibility checks and concrete counterexamples before freeze. Stop or revise weak candidates early; valid negative historical evaluations can end research on frozen claims quickly. Carry qualified experience into the next epoch with exposure intact. Reuse certified features and execute a small supported slice while retaining other ideas for later capability extensions; a universal grammar need not precede useful work.

Historical exploration and qualified evaluation remain useful immediately. Reserve scarce fresh/forward confirmation for selected claims under an ex-ante allocation and selection-aware evaluation plan; do not require years of waiting for every useful research step. Measure discovery and testing latency, cost and decision quality together, with end-to-end null/planted-effect calibration—not merely counts of winners or rejections.

This repository specifies these mechanisms but implements none. Numerical gates and relevant lifecycle, statistical and allocation decisions remain open. Weakening evidence rules or free invention would require a reviewable, approved material change; removing free invention is **GOAL CHANGE — USER APPROVAL REQUIRED**.
