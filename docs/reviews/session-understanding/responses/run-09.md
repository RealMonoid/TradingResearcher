The criticism identifies a real evidence bottleneck but draws an unjustified conclusion about research productivity.

What is right: convincingly establishing a modest trading edge can require substantial independent information. An annualized Sharpe of 1 alone does not determine the necessary calendar duration: power, confidence, return dependence, nonstationarity, selection, costs, and the precise claim matter. More bars, instruments, or repeated tests do not automatically provide independent observations. Exploratory memory is not independent confirmatory evidence. D014 and D019 explicitly preserve inherited exposure and allow heuristics to guide search without upgrading support. D023 retains forward evidence for clean confirmation; even forward data require valid stopping and selection rules.

What is wrong: the project aims to accelerate a research process, not merely wait for validated strategies. Its intended loop runs from permitted context through free discovery, faithful formalization and freeze, independent evaluation, and qualified feedback into the next epoch. Historical-first evaluation is accepted (D013; REJ-012); years of existing observations need not entail years of wall-clock waiting. Before stronger confirmation, bounded questions, feasibility checks, concrete counterexamples, historical evaluation, and retained failures can improve research decisions and eliminate poor candidates. A valid negative result can be useful; missing capabilities, technical failures, or low-power inconclusive results do not falsify a claim. Surviving pre-freeze criticism is not validation (D036).

The criticism also substitutes whole-strategy Sharpe validation for every market claim. Hypothesis evidence, separately evaluated policy performance, and framework success are different questions (D010, D025). Historical feedback may improve search without proving that improvement or making exposed history fresh.

What must be measured:

- Discovery and testing speed separately: wall-clock time, human effort, compute/token cost, and time to a reproducible decision, against a predefined comparable workflow with matched budgets and evidence access. Track unsupported ideas and queue delays as well as completed tests.
- Decision quality: end-to-end null-world false support, planted-effect detection and recovery, dependence sensitivity, and process-placebo results with uncertainty. Assess whether memory improves subsequent research on appropriately separated evaluation opportunities; neither confirmation counts nor rejection counts suffice.
- Evidence and economic yield: effective information, exposure overlap, confirmation waiting time, uncertainty and inconclusive rates, plus separately tested policy value after realistic costs and execution constraints.

These are evaluation dimensions, not newly approved thresholds. OPEN-01 leaves lifecycle semantics unresolved; OPEN-05 and OPEN-11 leave statistical and framework-success gates open. The repository contains documentation only, so rapid useful research remains an unverified target. Its present lack of a working loop reflects nonimplementation, not proof that forward waiting makes the intended loop idle.
