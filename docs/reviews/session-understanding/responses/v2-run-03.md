The criticism is right about evidence scarcity, but its conclusion uses the wrong success criterion. This project aims to accelerate both hypothesis discovery and testing, not merely produce a forward-confirmed profitable strategy. The relevant loop is permitted context → free invention → faithful formalization and freeze → independent historical evaluation → qualified experience for the next epoch; selected claims receive additional forward confirmation.

What is right: establishing a modest annualized Sharpe reliably can require years of informative observations. “Sharpe 1” alone does not determine the required duration: precision, power, dependence, selection, distributional assumptions and stability matter. Fifteen-minute observations or additional correlated instruments do not automatically supply independent evidence. Exploratory memory cannot establish empirical support or restore freshness. D014 and D019 preserve inherited exposure; D023 preserves clean forward confirmation. Forward outcomes also do not cure optional stopping or selection automatically.

What is wrong: years of historical observations need not require years of elapsed research time. Historical-first evaluation is accepted (D013; REJ-012). Before freeze, counterexamples and feasibility checks can stop or revise candidates. After freeze, sufficiently informative valid negative tests can eliminate claims; historical exploration and qualified evaluation can inform subsequent search. Technical failure, unsupported execution capability and inconclusiveness remain distinct from falsification. Surviving criticism is not validation (D036). These mechanisms permit useful progress while confirmation accumulates; they do not prove generator improvement, profitability or deployment readiness.

What must be measured:

- Discovery and testing latency separately, end-to-end decision time, compute/data/model costs and human repair effort. Compare against a named prior version or existing workflow with matched scope, evidence access and decision quality. Throughput or rejection counts alone cannot establish acceleration; this comparison does not authorize the rejected comparator generator.
- Decision quality: faithful formalization, reproducibility, technical validity, false-support rates, power and erroneous elimination of planted effects, with uncertainty and retained failures. Calibration must cover the adaptive discovery and selection process, not only the final statistical test (D025).
- Evidence consumption: exposure, overlap, effective information, confirmation waiting time and attainable precision for each frozen claim. Distinguish qualified historical findings from clean forward confirmation.
- Economic usefulness through separately evaluated policies, including costs, execution realism, downside and relevant alternatives. Hypothesis support is not policy validation.

The repository contains documentation, not an operating research loop. Acceleration and useful research performance are therefore specified objectives, not demonstrated capabilities. Statistical and framework success thresholds remain unresolved (OPEN-05, OPEN-11); lifecycle semantics remain OPEN-01. The criticism does not establish impossibility, and the architecture does not establish success.
