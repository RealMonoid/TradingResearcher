# Reference outcome materialization

`reference-cases/1` is a bounded deterministic computation interface implementing the unambiguous expression and historical-partition requirements in [research-contract/1.0 sections 4 and 6](../docs/architecture/14_RETAIL_RESEARCH_CONTRACT.md). Uncertain envelope membership remains a review gap under section 14. It is not a scientific EvaluationPlan, data-access grant, calibrated estimator or evidence classifier. It does not change the meaning of an original idea. The reference API is not exposed as a discovery tool and never emits the `EligibleOutcomeBatchCommitted` event or claims D087 invocation authority.

`materialize_reference(request, catalog, rows)` accepts exactly these request fields: `schema` (`reference-case-request/1`), `candidate_ref`, `data_snapshot_ref`, `scope_program`, `condition_program`, `outcome_program`, `outcome_scale` (`binary_probability` or `quantity`), `horizon_bars`, `universe` (`start`, `end`, `instruments`), `as_of`, and `max_steps`. Instants are exact UTC strings ending in Z. Programs bind the supplied catalog via `research-ast/1`. Scope and condition are Boolean. A binary outcome is Boolean and converted explicitly to 0/1; a quantity outcome remains a real with its original unit. Horizon is a positive number of 900-second observations. The caller's data/candidate references are retained, not authenticated by this pure calculator.

For every instrument and grid instant in the requested half-open universe, retain a case, including missing grid rows. Decision scope/condition are calculated with availability restricted to the decision instant. A new bar does not complete a longer-horizon outcome. Only the same instrument's exact future anchor `decision_time + horizon_bars * 900` may supply the outcome expression; no nearest-row substitution is allowed. Lag/window nodes inside that expression keep their existing meaning relative to the future anchor, so a return can be represented by future price divided by its explicitly lagged baseline, minus one.

An outcome is calculated only after its anchor is reached. Its availability cutoff is the earlier of `as_of` and the last second before the universe's exclusive end. Late publication may complete an outcome later than its anchor, but cannot import information published in the next partition. If the anchor reaches or crosses the end, retain `PARTITION_BOUNDARY` before calculating its outcome. A missing future row after its anchor is a data gap, not permission to substitute a later bar. Missing historical outcomes do not cause an indefinite wait for the clock.

Three-valued expression semantics remain unchanged: a determinable Boolean result can be retained even when a nondecisive operand is unknown. Such an outcome is `REVIEW_REQUIRED` when an inspected operand is unavailable or missing: this reference slice has not certified which inputs are required for completed-outcome authority. An outcome operand before the decision must demonstrably have been available by that decision; otherwise retain `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Outcome lookbacks before the universe start also retain `REVIEW_REQUIRED` pending certification of the exact warmup/partition meaning. These review gaps preserve the calculated value when one exists but block descriptive contrast. They neither redefine Boolean logic nor permanently reject the idea. Ordinary decision-program warmup still uses the original decision cutoff.

The case records both the earliest structurally declared outcome observation and the earliest actually inspected one, plus the latest availability of inspected, available outcome operands. A missing window member cannot hide a declared lookback across the universe boundary. For a fully observed, unambiguous outcome the label-information interval starts at the decision instant and ends at the later of the declared anchor and those publication times. For pending, missing or review cases this interval is null. A calculation trace describes supplied inputs; it does not prove source timestamp truth, independence, dependency certification or absence of dependence from overlapping lookbacks. Binary results use the `ratio` unit and admit only exact zero or one.

Processing states distinguish `ELIGIBLE`, `SCOPE_FALSE`, `SCOPE_UNKNOWN`, `NOT_YET_OBSERVED`, `OUTCOME_PENDING`, `OUTCOME_MISSING`, `DATA_GAP`, `PARTITION_BOUNDARY` and `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Here `ELIGIBLE` means only included in this reference contrast, never certified for scientific use. Cases are ordered by `(decision_time, instrument)`. A future decision is `NOT_YET_OBSERVED`, a missing decision row is `DATA_GAP`, and scope false/unknown exclusions precede horizon/boundary processing. The outcome is pending when the anchor or a still-admissible required publication has not arrived by `as_of`. Once the fixed partition cutoff prevents further admissible arrival, retain a missing/data reason instead. A present false binary outcome is zero, not missing. An unknown condition excludes the row from the conditional subset but leaves an otherwise eligible scope row in the whole-scope baseline, as declared by `scope-true-outcome-observed/1`.

Retain the request, each case, exclusion counts and processing/outcome readiness separately. A request is bounded to at most 100,000 expected grid cases and at most 10,000,000 primitive calculation steps. Its explicit `max_steps` can be lower. Exhaustion returns `RESOURCE_STOP` with retained completed cases and `processing_complete: false`; descriptive contrast refuses such partial output. These are software resource limits, not scientific stopping rules or a claim that correlated rows are independent observations.

`reference_contrast(report)` calculates the exact conditional-minus-whole-scope mean only after requested processing and outcome maturation are complete. It checks ordered universe coverage, consistent completion/review/count metadata, eligible-case maturity metadata and bounded normalized rationals. It reports both denominators and both means, and returns `RESOURCE_STOP` with null effect if exact sums, means or the final contrast exceed 8,192 bits. Zero eligible scope or conditioned cases returns an information gap with null effect. It never substitutes a complement baseline, emits a confidence interval, applies a practical-effect threshold or issues a market outcome. This pure API trusts its caller; structural validation does not authenticate a report or its source data. Real scientific use still requires authenticated input snapshots, independent feature/semantic certification, complete frozen allocation/evaluation rules and compatible calibration.
