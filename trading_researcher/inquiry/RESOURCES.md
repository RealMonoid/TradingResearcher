# One finite inquiry resource disposition

Version: inquiry-four-stage-resources/1. Authority: the direct discretionary
spending instruction retained in document 24, with the original stricter USD 5
shared inference ledger and all earlier charges. This is a new finite operator
allocation, not reuse of any of the six consumed and revoked grants. Workers
cannot create a successor allocation. No scientific meaning or evidence rule
changes. Installation and fixture checks do not activate a production epoch.

The separate FiniteInquiryBroker admits four ordered, single-use call slots:
discovery A, independent discovery B, faithful formalization of A's accepted
final idea, and a distinct worker's independent review of that exact accepted,
non-null STRUCTURE_CHECKED formalization. Intermediate ideas and B are retained
but cannot replace the preselected A target after failure. No retry or tool
follow-up call fits this contract. An unsupported input, incomplete response,
failed stage or exhausted resource returns a retained gap; never repair the
scientific content manually or silently switch to an easier idea.

Each call retains its existing 80,000-token ceiling. Input/output role maxima
are 74,000/6,000 twice, 48,000/32,000 and 76,000/4,000. Their explicit total is
320,000 tokens, replacing neither the ordinary 80,000-token contract nor an old
provisional cap. The measured 308,734-token path is not the worst-case ceiling.
The epoch has four calls, 1,800 seconds and at most 1,043,277,500 nanoUSD
(USD 1.0432775), within the original shared USD 5 cap. Role call timeouts remain
240/240/360/360 seconds; remaining epoch time must cover a full call timeout.

Declared later-stage input planning holds are 68,362/68,372/29,083/43,346 tokens
with full 6,000/6,000/32,000/4,000 output room. The last two inputs are historical
references, not guaranteed packet sizes, lower bounds or permission to omit
content. Before every dispatch, count the complete actual wire and reserve the
adapter's count-plus-128 bound within the original role limit. Actual inputs
may exceed a planning hold; dispatch stops if its complete reservation plus
later holds cannot be funded. All role maxima together cost USD 1.16, exceeding
the residual allowance. This is conditional continuation, not a funded promise
that an arbitrary complete research cycle will succeed.

Only genuinely unspent inference reservation can become available again.
Spent charges, consumed call slots and prior grants never reset. Before a later
stage, reconcile the native response with its exact wire hash, prebound dispatch
identity, epoch/worker/model/prompt metadata and durable shared-ledger receipt,
including identical known usage and conservative published-rate charge. Missing,
unknown, mismatched, overrun, late or source-stale accounting stops dependent
work. Worker-reported cheap usage alone cannot release funds. Settlement retains
the response and known accounting before committing a halt. Ledger provenance
trusts the local operator, installed code and OS; it is not remote attestation.

One immutable external grant binds the registry path, epoch/request, installed
source closure, shared ledger and complete role profiles. Its ordered fence
survives registry rollback. A missing native call, missing provider dispatch,
restored registry, different registry or reused approval fails closed; it does
not recover a fresh slot. Revocation is permanent. The existing ledger protects
the total cash cap atomically; unrelated concurrent spending may invalidate
future holds, in which case admission stops rather than promising future funds.

The operator freezes complete actual role profiles and distinct discovery run
identities. Discovery uses the existing low-reasoning profile; formalization
keeps high reasoning, full sources and 32,000 output tokens; review preserves
the exact full independent meaning-review protocol, low reasoning and 4,000
output tokens. A historical hypothesis-specific horizon instruction is not
reused for a new idea. The auxiliary dispatcher selects credentials and shared
storage only from the installed route, and selects configuration only from the
frozen role profile. Counting occurs outside the registry transaction; complete
source/lease/request currentness is rechecked before native reservation.

Count-endpoint billing remains unconfirmed and separate from inference usage.
No count size is a billing receipt or zero-charge proof. Model-produced inquiry
behavior, semantic prompt qualification, actual future packet fit, native
source/context admission, scientific calibration, policy economics and benefit
remain separately governed and unverified by fixture resource checks.

Rollback disables this new entry point for future work and revokes a created
grant, retaining all sources, ideas, attempts, receipts, charges and exposure.
